**Where the real bottleneck is:** heavy rare earths (Dy/Tb) and, second, ex-China finished-magnet capacity. Precision motor/reducer manufacturing is a Western bottleneck but a Chinese commodity. Cell energy density is a product-performance constraint that every major cell maker is attacking; supply is not scarce.

## (b) Per-component detail

### 1. NdFeB magnets and heavy rare earths

**China export-control status (Sep 2026)**
- China suspended its 9 Oct 2025 package (the extraterritorial/0.1% de-minimis rules) for one year, **until 10 Nov 2026** ([Clark Hill](https://www.clarkhill.com/news-events/news/china-hits-pause-on-rare-earth-export-controls-and-what-it-means-for-supply-chains/)).
- The **April 2025 licensing regime on seven heavy REEs (Dy, Tb, Sm, Gd, Lu, Sc, Y) and any NdFeB magnet containing Dy/Tb was never suspended**; Chinese customs data show Dy/Tb exports ~50% below the pre-restriction baseline ([CSIS](https://www.csis.org/analysis/rare-earth-export-restrictions-one-year-later); [TechTimes, May 2026](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/317208/20260526/china-rare-earth-export-controls-april-curbs-still-bite-after-beijing-summit.htm); [TRADIUM](https://tradium.com/market-insight/china-dysprosium-exports-rise-while-terbium-shipments-remain-well-below-last-years-levels/)).
- Analysts expect China's HRE grip to last "well into the 2030s"; the two November-2026 expiry dates are the near-term catalyst ([China Briefing](https://www.china-briefing.com/news/chinas-rare-earth-export-controls-impacts-on-businesses/)).

**Pricing (2026)**
- NdPr oxide ~US$144/kg (early 2026) ([critical-minerals-news](https://critical-minerals-news.com/rare-earths-price/)); MP's DoD floor is US$110/kg.
- Tb oxide US$1,097/kg (SMM China domestic, 3 Sep 2026); Dy oxide US$239/kg domestic (3 Sep 2026) ([rare-earth-mining.com](https://rare-earth-mining.com/terbium-price/)).
- **Ex-China premium:** Dy trades US$212–218/kg inside China vs. US$575–2,500+/kg for non-Chinese buyers; Tb shows a 4–5x premium ([Rare Earth Exchanges](https://rareearthexchanges.com/news/dysprosium-terbium-china-ex-china-price-divide/)). This two-tier market is the clearest quantitative evidence of the bottleneck.

**Magnet content per humanoid**
- Published estimates: 2–4.5 kg NdFeB per robot; most common figure 3.5–4 kg, roughly 2x an EV ([FAI](https://www.thefai.org/posts/the-robot-boom-has-a-magnet-problem); [Stockhead](https://stockhead.com.au/resources/how-the-dawn-of-the-robot-age-could-send-rare-earth-demand-ballistic/)). Optimus ~3.5 kg across 40+ motors; 50–100k units → 175–350 t/yr ([Rare Earth Exchanges](https://rareearthexchanges.com/news/tesla-optimus-ndfeb-magnet-supply-chain/)). Every 10,000 units (30–40 DoF) ≈ 30+ t ([Rare Earth Exchanges](https://rareearthexchanges.com/news/humanoid-robots-ndpr-magnet-demand/)). Magnets ≈ 8% of BOM. Robotics NdFeB demand CAGR ~28% to 2040 (Adamas, via [Stockhead](https://stockhead.com.au/resources/how-the-dawn-of-the-robot-age-could-send-rare-earth-demand-ballistic/)).
- Dy/Tb dependence: joint motors run hot; grain-boundary diffusion (GBD) cuts Dy/Tb by 50–70% and has been applied to radial multipole rings used in joint motors ([Bunting](https://www.magnetapplications.com/blog/grain-boundary-diffusion-of-heavy-rare-earth-elements-hree-in-sintered-ndfeb-magnets); [FAI](https://www.thefai.org/posts/the-robot-boom-has-a-magnet-problem)). Zhenghai is pushing HRE-free grades for EVs/humanoids ([Rare Earth Exchanges](https://rareearthexchanges.com/news/chinese-rare-earth-magnet-makers-are-building-for-a-different-future-even-as-chinas-market-faces-new-friction/)).

**Humanoid makers' disclosed magnet sourcing**
- **Tesla:** Only disclosure is negative — Musk (Apr 2025) said Optimus production was "affected" by China's controls and Tesla was seeking export licences ([The Hill](https://thehill.com/business/5262814-tesla-optimus-robots-rare-earth-magnets/); [Global Times](https://www.globaltimes.cn/page/202504/1332704.shtml)). No public confirmation of resolution as of March 2026. Diversification "reportedly" involves MP, Lynas, Noveon [UNVERIFIED] ([rare-earth-mining.com](https://rare-earth-mining.com/tesla-rare-earth/)).
- **Figure, Agility, Apptronik, 1X, Unitree, UBTech, Boston Dynamics, Agibot:** No magnet supplier disclosures found [NOT FOUND]. Unitree makes motors in-house and sources domestically.

### 2. Motors (frameless, axial-flux, QDD)

**Who supplies whom**
- **Tesla Optimus:** Sanhua Intelligent Controls (002050.SZ) is the exclusive Tier-1 for actuator *assemblies* (reported ~RMB 5 bn/US$685 m order, Oct 2025, delivering from Mexico in 2026); Tuopu (601689.SS) is Tier-1 for linear actuators and is reportedly supplying frameless torque motors and rotary joint modules (reported RMB 3 bn rotating-joint order for 2026). Both are building Thai plants. Rotary joints = frameless torque motor + harmonic reducer + torque/position sensors ([36kr](https://eu.36kr.com/en/p/3728136166797832); [Futu](https://news.futunn.com/en/post/36178166/two-major-a-share-tesla-suppliers-simultaneously-increase-the-robot); [optimusk](https://optimusk.blog/blog/tesla-optimus-suppliers/)). **The motor itself is Tesla-designed; the magnet supplier is undisclosed.**

- **Unitree:** fully in-house QDD PMSM motors (1:6–1:9 planetary), G1 up to 120 N·m; no external motor logo on teardown; motors cost 30–40% of Western equivalents ([SemiAnalysis](https://newsletter.semianalysis.com/p/chinas-unitree-will-dominate-global); [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/320711/20260716/unitree-robotics-nears-shanghai-ipo-profitable-actuators-exposed-chinas-spy-law.htm)).

- **LG group:** positioning LG Innotek/LG Electronics actuators + LGES batteries as a "One LG" package; LG bipedal humanoid with Nvidia due Q1 2027 ([Korea Herald](https://www.koreaherald.com/article/10710964); [KED](https://www.kedglobal.com/robotics/newsView/ked202608140008)).
