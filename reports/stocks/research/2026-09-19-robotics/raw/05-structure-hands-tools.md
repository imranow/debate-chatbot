**Bottlenecked (my ranking):** (1) precision thread/profile grinders and the grinding know-how for planetary roller screws; (2) roller screws themselves (lead times 26+ weeks); (3) harmonic reducers (20-40 per robot) until Schaeffler/Chinese capacity lands 2027; (4) PEEK polymer if volumes reach 1M units. **Commoditised:** die-castings, tendon fibre, standard connectors, assembly/EMS, low-DoF hands (<$1,500), thermal hardware.

## (b) Detail with sourced figures

### 1. Dexterous hands

**Tesla Optimus Gen 3 / V3 hand**
- 22 DoF in the hand plus wrist DoF; three tendons per finger run from forearm actuators through the wrist into channels inside the phalanges (Teslarati / tesery patent write-ups). Tesla moved "all 25 actuators per arm into the forearm" using coreless motors driving tendons (droids.substack; basenor).
- Cable bundles exit the forearm in a lateral stack and are re-ordered into a vertical stack at the wrist to reduce friction, stretch and torque crosstalk (patent summary, tesery).
- Cost: Morgan Stanley's Gen-2 teardown put hands at ~17% of BOM (~$9,500 of a $50-60k unit) (optimusk.blog citing MS). Tesla's stated mass-production BOM target is ~$20,000/unit with ~28 actuators (rotary + linear) and ~20 coreless motors for hands (36kr; optimusk.blog).
- Production status: Optimus entered production at Fremont after Model S/X line teardown (final S/X early May 2026; 46-day conversion — Electrek 22 Apr 2026; Yahoo). Musk: production "late July or August 2026", output "quite slow", "10,000 unique parts"; >1,000 Gen-3 units operating at Fremont (Jan 2026 claim); realistic 2026 output "low thousands" (optimusk.blog). Sales to public targeted 2027.

**Tendon fibre suppliers**
- 1.5 mm UHMWPE braided tendon: ~300 N breaking load at 1.2 g/m; UHMWPE/aramid braids are 5-8× steel wire strength-to-weight (corematter.substack). Named tendon-rope suppliers: Avient (Dyneema, acquired from DSM 2022), Honeywell (Spectra), Toyobo (pmarketresearch). Materials content per hand is grams — immaterial to AVNT/HON revenue; moat is technical, not commercial.

**Micro motors / drives**
- Zhaowei (003021.SZ): supplies Tesla micro precision transmission components for dexterous hands; four generations of hand products; 25 years in micro-transmission; building a USD100m plant in Thailand (Yicai). **Financial reality check:** 2025 robotics segment revenue only 23.87m CNY (~$3.5m), 1.4% of total revenue (Gasgoo/Futu). Risk: valuation embeds Tesla optionality far ahead of revenue.
- Leadshine (CN, private): DH116 hand, 490 g incl. sensors, brushless coreless motors + worm gear, shown at CES 2026 (PRNewswire 7 Jan 2026).
- Portescap (Regal Rexnord RRX): coreless DC since 1959, markets BLDC/coreless motors for humanoid robots; Maxon, Faulhaber, Portescap "long occupied high share" in coreless (SMM/MIR). Risk for RRX: humanoid is immaterial to a ~$6bn-revenue group **[unverified]**.
- Hengli Hydraulic (601100.SS): screw samples passed Tesla validation and entered small-batch delivery; planetary roller screw mass production target >5,000 units/month by 2026-27; "ranks first among competitors on capacity progress" (36kr). Micro-screws recognised as core hand components; one source argues roller-screw count per hand "may be doubled" as hands move to high-load designs (kggfa). Xinjian Transmission IPO: 2.8bn CNY plan targeted at Tesla, but ball-screw revenue <10% of sales (BigGo).

**Chinese hand makers**
- Inspire Robots: delivered 10,000 hands in 2025; capacity 30-50k/yr (sourcebotics). Linkerbot: SE model <$1,500 up to 42-DoF L30 research flagship, published prices. Sharpa Wave: 22 active DoF with high-res tactile. Brainco: cited for weight-to-load and teleoperation strengths. 2026 pricing: entry five-finger <$1,500; flagship 20+ DoF tactile hands $7,000-28,000 (sourcebotics). China dexterous hand market forecast 960m CNY in 2026, +170% YoY (Huaan Securities via Futu). One hand startup's valuation reportedly "nears" Unitree's (BigGo headline; name not retrievable).
- Sanctuary AI (private): 21-DoF hydraulic hand; total funding ~$140m incl. C$30m Canadian SIF; in-hand cube reorientation 10/10 demos using Isaac Lab (Robot Report). Shadow Robot **[NO DATA retrieved this session]**.

### 2. Structural materials and parts
- **Tuopu 601689.SS**: Tesla chassis supplier since 2016 → "exclusive rotary actuator supplier for Optimus" per Futu/Poems commentary; first-batch orders 1.5bn CNY; target ~60% of Optimus reducer supply by 2026; also dexterous-hand motors under development; market cap ~$15.2bn, +33% YTD (Futu). Biggest risk: Tesla ramp slippage (V3 production already pushed twice in 2026).
- **Xusheng 603305.SS**: semi-solid die-casting applied to robotics; produces integrated joint housings and torso structural parts; magnesium body shells for **Figure AI** (2025) (aluminiumchina / innovaw). Risk: Figure insources at BotQ.
- **Wencan 603348.SS**: 9,800 T two-platen line at Chongqing cut humanoid skeleton manufacturing cycle 72h→18h (−75%) (empcasting).
- **Magnesium**: GRINM high-magnesium aluminium alloys for humanoids (rareearthexchanges); a Mg die-cast-body humanoid reported by Foundry-Planet **[details unreachable]**. Ryobi **[NO DATA]**.
- **Carbon fibre**: Zhongfu Shenying (688295.SS) first industrial mass production of T1200 (SYT80, >8,000 MPa) (Vizon); Toray >29,000 t/yr capacity, also developing T1200; Hexcel primary US military aero supplier. CF used in humanoid joints, limbs, skeleton; trade press asks whether robots can become a "volume market" for CF post Unitree's $62bn IPO (jotaintl). Verdict: immaterial to Toray/Hexcel P&L; Zhongfu is the thematic play.
- **PEEK**: Victrex >50% of global PEEK capacity; Zhongyan largest CN producer, #4 global, second worldwide with a 5,000 L polymerisation reactor; Pengfulong and ARKPEEK cited as challengers (peekchina). Demand claim: by 2027 total capacity meets only ~1M-robot demand (~11,000 t) (peekchina). Zhongyan capacity +30% in 2023 on UBTech/Xiaomi demand. PEEK harmonic drives are an emerging use case (Junhua). Victrex/Syensqo/Evonik FY26 humanoid disclosures **[NO DATA]**.
- **3D printing**: BLT FY2025 revenue 1.852bn CNY (+39.7%), net profit 204m (+95.1%); Farsoon FY2025 revenue 715m CNY (+45.4%) (faxiangongchang snippet). BLT exhibits metal-printed humanoid parts and co-develops multi-axis force sensors with Haptron; BLT–Siemens strategic agreement at TCT Asia 2026; Farsoon fine-laser-spot metal PBF (3druck). ### 3. Precision machine tools
- McKinsey: planetary roller screws are potentially "an even more acute supply bottleneck than harmonic gearboxes" (narrow supplier base, long lead times, micron tolerances, few substitutes) (ignoretheconfusion; Europe Capital). Roller-screw lead times "26 weeks or longer", raw-material delays add 12-18 weeks; grinding/heat-treatment equipment tolerances <5 µm; Nippon Thompson (6480.T) and Rollvis (private) "operating near capacity" (medicineandmarkets / Europe Capital snippets).
- Chinese demand signal: "screw factories buying grinding machines, reducer factories buying inspection equipment, dexterous-hand factories looking for suppliers" across Yangtze/Pearl River Delta (faxiangongchang, 2026).
- Chinese equipment makers: Huachen Precision 300809.SZ (national "little giant" in precision grinding, ChiNext-listed Dec 2019); Qinchuan 000837.SZ supplies "magnetic gear, precision grinding, advancing roller screws", batch supply targeted 2026 (faxiangongchang / interestingengineering). Haomai 002595.SZ, Nissin **[NO DATA — search budget exhausted]**.
- Western/Japanese: Reishauer and Kapp Niles named as thread/profile grinder makers (Kapp Niles ~1,000 employees 2025). DMG Mori/Okuma/Makino: no humanoid-specific order data found **[NO DATA]**. Schaeffler announced a new strain-wave gearbox manufacturing process in Aug 2026, mass manufacturing from 2027 (jamesm.blog) — a potential de-bottlenecking event for harmonic gears.
- Harmonic Drive Systems (6324.T): FY3/26 sales guidance raised ¥57bn→¥59bn, OP ¥1.5bn→¥2.5bn; ~¥1.3bn humanoid orders in one quarter, "considerable level" from FY3/27 (Finimize / topsecretstocks). 20-40 harmonic reducers per humanoid (etfdb).
- **Is the grinder the true bottleneck?** Evidence supports "yes for the next 12-24 months": (i) the constraint cited by McKinsey and practitioners is precision grinding + heat treatment, not raw screw blanks; (ii) Chinese screw makers are visibly buying grinders; (iii) Western grinder makers are private, capacity-constrained firms. But **no source gave a 2026 delivery lead time for a Reishauer/Kapp Niles/Huachen thread grinder** — this is the single most important missing datapoint and should be obtained directly (GrindingHub 2026 coverage, company IR). CBN abrasives (Noritake, 3M, Element Six) **[NO DATA]**; treat as consumable, non-bottleneck.

### 4. Cables, connectors, slip rings
- TE Connectivity: Motorman hybrid connector (power+signal+data in one housing) cuts housing size/cable requirements up to 50% and cable length up to 70% (usa-automation / TE). Hirose: BH12 hybrid with two 20 A power contacts + signal, 13.8 mm plug, IPX6/IPX7; solution map by head/upper body/torso/lower body (Hirose). Molex: Mirror Mezz, Quad-Row, FAKRA-Mini, Mega-Fit, EXTreme Ten60; humanoid-specific hybrid production "late 2026" (Molex; Automation USA). Amphenol, JAE, Luxshare, Chinese harness makers, slip rings, and $ harness content per robot **[NO DATA]**. Tesla is using Optimus itself for "cable routing, connector seating" tasks at Fremont (optimusk), implying non-trivial harness complexity.

### 5. Thermal management
- Sanhua (002050.SZ): Tesla Tier-1 since 2017; humanoid actuator work with Tesla since 2023; downsized automotive liquid-cooling into robot joints; 3.8bn CNY Qiantang (Hangzhou) base targeting 1M actuators/yr by 2026; Jiemian flagged a "Tesla order rumour" that fuelled a rally; a "$5bn Tesla robot order" headline (zhaochips) is **unverified**. Tesla procured high-performance fluorinated fluid from Yonghe Shares for robot liquid cooling (moomoo). Academic work on evaporative/liquid-cooled actuators (arXiv, ScienceDirect 2026 hydrogel evaporative cooling) suggests thermal remains an active design frontier. Delta, Auras, Boyd humanoid specifics **[NO DATA]**.

### 6. Contract manufacturing / assembly
- Foxconn + NVIDIA Houston AI-server plant: humanoids (Isaac GR00T N) deployed on production line from Q1 2026, tasks = picking, placing, cable insertion; Nvidia's first humanoid use in manufacturing (Reuters via Yahoo; Assembly; The Register 7 Nov 2025).
- Jabil–Apptronik: Jabil manufacturing partner for Apollo; deal followed Apptronik's $350m Series A; Jabil to build in its own factories once commercially viable; commercial units targeted 2026 (Drives&Controls, TechCrunch).
- Figure: 350+ Figure 03 delivered by Apr 2026, output one robot per hour at BotQ; Figure 03 deployed in Hall 52 Spartanburg after Figure 02 supported 30,000 X3s (BMW press, 26 Jun 2026).
- Agility: RoboFab designed for up to 10,000/yr; Digit 5 unveiled 15 Sep 2026; >$300m multi-year orders, 30+ customer pipeline; going public via Churchill Capital XI SPAC at $2.5bn (7 Jul 2026, R&AN).
- Quanta, Wistron, Pegatron, Flex, Luxshare humanoid deals **[NO DATA]**. Takeaway: leading Western OEMs (Tesla, Figure, Agility) insource final assembly; EMS wins are second-tier (Apptronik/Jabil) or deployment-as-customer (Foxconn).
