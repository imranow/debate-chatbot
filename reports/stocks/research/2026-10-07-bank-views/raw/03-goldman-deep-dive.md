## 1. AI

- Framework: four phases. Phase 1 = Nvidia. Phase 2 = AI infrastructure (semis, cloud, data-center REITs, hardware, security software, utilities). Phase 3 = firms that sell AI-enabled revenue (mostly software, IT services). Phase 4 = firms with the biggest AI productivity boost to earnings. Source: Goldman US equity note (Kostin et al.), 2024 vintage, hosted by Spirepoint. https://www.spirepointpc.com/documents/FG/spirepoint/resource-center/637675_US_Equity_Views__AI_and_US_Equities__Growing_into_AI_Phase_2_multiples_and_staying_selective_within_AI_Phase_3_stocks.pdf (OLD, 2024)
- July 23, 2026 (Goldman Insights, Ben Snider): AI infrastructure volatility pushes investors to three non-AI themes with low correlation to AI: consumer-experience firms, "compounders", M&A candidates. Snider: hard for fund managers to hold bullish AI-infrastructure views in portfolios. Expects only modest hyperscaler capex revisions; too early for 2027 guidance. https://www.goldmansachs.com/insights/articles/three-new-stock-themes-are-in-focus-as-the-ai-trade-gyrates
- September 2026 (Ben Snider, US equity strategy; summary of a 17 Sep 2026 note): no earnings bubble. Base case is slower profit growth, not a collapse. Forward S&P 500 P/E fell from 23x a year ago to 19x, near its 10-year average. Almost half of 2026 S&P 500 EPS growth comes from AI investment. https://www.goldmansachs.com/insights/articles/can-ai-investment-drive-s-and-p-500-earnings-even-higher ; https://www.htx.com/news/goldman-sachs-research-report-analysis-exceptionally-strong-m6Sej7cq/
- Hyperscaler capex (same Sep 2026 work): about $800bn in 2026 (+94% vs 2025), $1.2tn in 2027 (+54%, about $130bn above consensus), $1.4tn in 2028 (+12%). By 2028 depreciation fully offsets the capex profit boost. https://www.briefs.co/news/goldman-says-hyperscaler-ai-spend-could-jump-to-1-2-trillion/ ; https://www.techflowpost.com/en-US/article/34264 (secondary; figures (unverified) against the primary note)
- Snider also flags chip margin pressure ahead and an 8pp drag on 2027 S&P 500 EPS growth from the full removal of "other income" in 2027 vs 2026. The snippet does not say what that income is. Date not shown; likely Sep 2026. https://uk.finance.yahoo.com/news/ai-capex-boom-wont-sustain-093002410.html (unverified)
- Conflicting capex number: a Goldman economics note, "More AI capex, more volatility", implies about $1.1tn in 2027 (+45%), upside case $1.4tn. Another Goldman graphic: $765bn (2026) rising to $1.64tn (2031), $7.6tn cumulative. Scopes differ. Dates not found. (unverified)

### Power and utilities (AI Phase 2)
- 4 Mar 2026 (GS Research, power/utilities team): global data-center power demand now +220% by 2030 vs 2023 (was +175%; first call in 2024/25 was +165%). https://www.goldmansachs.com/insights/goldman-sachs-research/how-rising-hyperscaler-reinvestment-impacts-power-growth-constraints (OLDER: Mar 2026)
- 20 May 2026 (GS Research): US data-center demand more than doubles, 31 GW (2025) to 66 GW (2027). Capacity adds 13.6 GW (2026), 36.3 GW (2027). Only 50-60% of near-term scheduled capacity likely on time. Data centers rise to 8.5% of US summer peak demand in 2027 (4.1% in 2025). Reliability risk highest in Mid-Atlantic, Mid-Continent, Northwest. https://www.marcus.com/us/en/resources/heard-at-gs/what-is-the-forecast-for-us-data-center-power-demand- ; https://www.goldmansachs.com/insights/articles/us-data-center-power-demand-projected-to-double-by-2027 (OLDER: May 2026)
- Older context: about $720bn of grid spend needed through 2030; about 60% of new demand met by gas (2025 work). https://goldmansachs.com/insights/articles/ai-to-drive-165-increase-in-data-center-power-demand-by-2030 (OLD)

### What it implies for stocks (Goldman's own framing)
- AI infrastructure (Phase 2) is still the earnings engine for 2026 but the boost fades in 2027-28 as capex growth slows and depreciation rises.
- Goldman's July-Sep 2026 tilt: keep AI exposure selective; add non-AI, low-correlation themes (consumer experience, compounders, M&A targets).
- Power: demand forecasts keep rising, but delivery delays cap near-term capacity. That is a support for gas, grid and generation names. Goldman names here: not found in snippets.

## 3. Obesity / GLP-1

- Latest: global anti-obesity drug sales $114bn in 2030 (was $101bn, set Dec 2025). Analysts: Asad Haider, James Quigley. Date: 2026, exact day not found (after the Wegovy pill launch and Lilly's Foundayo launch; probably Q2-Q3 2026). https://www.zerohedge.com/medical/goldman-hikes-obesity-drug-market-forecast-oral-glp-1s-go-mass-market (single outlet; unverified)
  - Ex-US 2030 sales $48bn (was $39bn). This drives most of the rise.
  - Orals 40% of 2030 sales, $46bn (was 35%, $35bn). Oral new-to-brand scripts now 40-50%.
  - Oral share: Lilly Foundayo 48% (was 54%), Novo Wegovy pill 38% (was 16%). Wegovy pill peak sales $17.4bn.
  - US direct-to-consumer price assumption cut 20%, $355 to $287 a month.
  - Lilly plus Novo hold 82% of global share by 2030. Haider: "This market is fragmenting... around types of therapy."
- Revision history: $100bn (2024 first call) -> $130bn (2024) -> $95bn (May 2025 cut; price erosion, tighter insurer coverage) -> $101bn (Dec 2025) -> $114bn (2026). https://www.goldmansachs.com/insights/articles/anti-obesity-drug-market ; https://www.goldmansachs.com/insights/articles/the-anti-obesity-drug-market-may-prove-smaller-than-expected ; https://www.itiger.com/hans/news/2526504266 (Barron's)
- Stock read-through (my inference, not Goldman's words): more volume, lower price, more pills. Goldman's own oral-share revision is better for Novo than its prior view. A larger oral share is a relative negative for injectable-pen supply chains.
