# QA review: bank views note (7 Oct 2026)

Note reviewed: `bank-views-oct-2026.md`. Checked against raw files 01–05 and, for section 7, the robotics (`projection-2030.md`, `robotics-supply-chain-moats.md` section 9), GLP-1 pen and egg-freezing notes. Four web searches were used to settle conflicts between raw files (10-year yield, UBS unit, Goldman P/E). No repo file other than this one was changed.

## (a) Verdict

**PASS WITH FIXES.**

Most figures trace to the raw files, and the bank-by-bank target table is correct. But there are 12 major defects. They are:
- the 10-year yield picture is out of date and misquotes Wilson;
- the UBS target is not attributed to its unit;
- a JPMorgan regional view mixes up its units;
- two derived claims are wrong ("as large as the whole gap", "almost 2 times");
- one factual claim is wrong ("Morgan Stanley and Citi publish only 2050 figures");
- the banks' reasons for their robot ratings are made up;
- one "Agree?" call is unfair (West);
- about ten "(unverified)" caveats were dropped.

There are no critical defects. All fixes are text edits.
