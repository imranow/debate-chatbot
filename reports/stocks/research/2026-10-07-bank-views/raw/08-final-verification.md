# Final verification: bank-views note (8 Oct 2026)

A multi-agent workflow checked the note frozen at commit 05aacd3. One fact-checker reviewed each section. A completeness critic read the whole note. Four news sweepers looked for facts since 1 October 2026. Every defect they found went to three independent verifiers (source, logic and reader lenses), and a defect was kept only if at least two of the three confirmed it. One editor per note then merged the kept defects into one consistent edit list and re-read the result.

## Changelog

The §7 Hims row was wrong to say no big bank rates Hims a Buy: JPMorgan is Overweight at $33 (May, unverified). The verdict is now Mixed, and the §8 conflicts line says so too. Goldman's EPS now uses the reported September figures ($415 for 2027). That shrinks the $35 gap with JPMorgan and Deutsche Bank to $5 and moves §1's growth ranges to about 23–36% and 11–17%. The 10-year yield story now runs to 6 October, when yields were near their highest since 2002. The Goldman gap is now about 0.9 points, and §4 adds the other banks' yield forecasts and Wilson's milder latest comments. Stale or undated items now carry their dates: the nine-bank target cluster, Bank of America's −3%, the track-record studies, the regional calls, Goldman's GLP-1 figure and several §7 ratings. Headings that claimed more than the sources support were toned down: AI as "most" of the bull case, power "not money", earnings "drive the market", UBS as "main counterweight" and "targets chase prices". Missing views were added: Subramanian's reasons for the lone bear call, Morgan Stanley's Fed call, the October inflation trigger and futures pricing, Morgan Stanley IM's US overweight, Lynas Sell ratings, index concentration and Dimon's view that AI will pay off. Dropped caveats were restored, jargon was defined for lay readers, and the first-person buy prices in the West and Progyny rows now say "our note sees value only after a big drawdown", with West's levels marked illustrative, not price targets. The header and §9 now describe the sourcing correctly. The sister Hims note still says no JPMorgan rating was found and needs the same fix.
