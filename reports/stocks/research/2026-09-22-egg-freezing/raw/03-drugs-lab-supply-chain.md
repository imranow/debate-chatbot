# Egg Freezing and IVF Supply Chain: Who Gets Paid Per Cycle (as of 2026-09-22)

**Bottom line:** Drug makers take about 85-90% of the money suppliers earn from an egg-freezing cycle, but their US prices are falling. The TrumpRx deal with EMD Serono (Oct 2025) cut list prices by 84%. Organon's fertility sales fell 9% ex-FX in Q1 2026 because of US price competition, and Merck's Gonal-f fell 6.7% organically in 2025.

The best-quality exposure to egg freezing specifically is vitrification. Kitazato (TSE: 368A) is the chokepoint: about 65% of its sales come from vitrification, its operating margin is about 53%, and it trades at about 13x P/E. After that come IVF lab consumables (Vitrolife, CooperSurgical).

Egg freezing uses far fewer lab consumables than IVF. There is no ICSI, no embryo culture, no time-lapse imaging and no PGT until the woman later thaws her eggs. So the thesis pays the drug makers and Kitazato now, and the IVF-stage suppliers years later.

Sizing context: SART reports 38,126 US oocyte-banking cycles for fertility preservation in 2023, about 9% of 425,869 total ART cycles. That is up from 6,090 in 2014 and 24,560 in 2021.

| Lab consumables (supplier revenue, **already inside the clinic fee**) | ~300-800 (**analyst estimate, not verified**): retrieval needle, media, oil, dishes, hyaluronidase, vitrification kit, about 4-8 Cryotop-type devices for ~10-15 eggs | ~500 | Kitazato, Vitrolife, CooperSurgical, Irvine Scientific | Estimate; no supplier publishes per-cycle revenue |
| Storage | Year 1 often bundled; then 500-1,500 per year | 800/yr | Clinic or storage company (ReproTech/Cooper, TMRW); equipment from MVE, Thermo, IC Biomedical; LN2 from Linde, Air Liquide | Clinic cost guides |
| **All-in first cycle** | **~11,000-20,000** | **~14,000-17,000** (national average cited ≈ $16,800 including drugs and one year of storage) | | Clinic cost guides |

How the supplier money divides in one egg-freezing cycle is my own estimate:
- Drugs: about $2.5-4.5k, roughly 85-90% of supplier revenue.
- Lab consumables: about $0.3-0.8k.
- Cryo equipment and LN2: under about $50 per specimen-year.

Most patients need more than one retrieval, so the total journey cost can reach $11k-32k or more.

The later IVF stage adds the higher-value consumables and services. ICSI and embryo culture add several hundred dollars. PGT-A typically adds $3-6k, which is my estimate and not verified in this search.

## (c) Detail by component (sourced)

### 1. Ovarian stimulation drugs

**Merck KGaA / EMD Serono (MRK.DE)**
- **Size:** Fertility revenue was about US$1.5bn in 2024, of which about US$375m (25%) came from the US (European Biotechnology Magazine).
- **FY2025:** Franchise organic growth was +0.4%. Gonal-f fell 6.7% organically and Pergoveris grew 22.6% (Merck FY2025 release). Q2 2025 was -3.4% organic, with Gonal-f -12.5%; Q3 2025 was +2.4%.
- **2026:** Fertility & Endocrinology was about -5% organic in Q1 and about -1% in Q2 2026 (Q2 call). Management cited Pergoveris sales of "over €300m" with a 5-year CAGR above 20% and a China launch. It is unclear whether the €300m is year-to-date or annual, so treat it as ambiguous.
- **TrumpRx deal (announced 16 Oct 2025):**
  - Direct-to-consumer sales of Gonal-f, Ovidrel and Cetrotide.
  - 84% off list price when all three are used in one protocol.
  - TrumpRx.gov went live in January 2026.
  - Separately, new guidance lets employers offer standalone fertility benefits.
  - Pergoveris was filed under FDA's Commissioner's National Priority Voucher programme (1-2 month review target), with a possible US launch in H2 2026. **I could not confirm US approval as of September 2026.** EMD Serono issued a further US access release on 6 Feb 2026.
- **Supply chain:** Tres Cantos (Madrid) is the main site making Gonal-f active substance for the whole world. It was expanded +50% with €15m over 2014-16. That makes it a single-site concentration for the active ingredient. The fill-finish and pen location is not verified.
- **Biggest risk:** US price erosion spreading to other buyers, plus biosimilars ex-US.

**Ferring (private)**
- Group revenue topped €2.5bn in 2025 for the first time: +7% reported, +10% at constant currency.
- Reproductive Medicine exceeded €1bn for the first time, led by Menopur.
- Ferring increased production of the Menopur pen and Rekovelle.
- Fertility Bridge says Menopur alone did "just over $1bn". That conflicts with the €1bn figure for the whole division, so treat it as unreliable.
- Menopur and Follistim are **not** on TrumpRx.
- Menopur is urine-derived (hMG), which is a different supply chain from recombinant FSH. That point is from my background knowledge and was not verified here.
- **Biggest risk:** pressure to match TrumpRx pricing in the US.

**Organon (OGN)**
- FY2025: Follistim AQ $264m (+11% ex-FX, helped by a one-time buy-in). Ganirelix $101m (-8%).
- Q4 2025: Follistim $58m (-12%), ganirelix $24m (-15%).
- Q1 2026: global fertility -9% ex-FX, "competition-driven price reductions in the U.S." This is consistent with TrumpRx pushing prices down.
- Sun Pharma agreed on 26-27 Apr 2026 to buy Organon at $14/share in cash (EV about $11.75bn). Closing is expected in early 2027. Organon had about 4.3x net leverage at the end of 2025 and sold JADA for up to $465m.

**Gedeon Richter (RICHT.BD)**
- Women's Health grew +14.8% in 2025 and +22% in Q4, driven mainly by Ryeqo and Drovelis. Bemfola is described as a protected brand.
- In May 2025 Richter took an equity stake and signed a co-development deal with Granata Bio to bring Bemfola to the US.
- **Missing:** Bemfola sales in HUF or EUR.

**China**
- Livzon (000513.SZ): NMPA approval for follitropin alfa "Li You Bao".
- GenSci / Changchun High-Tech (000661.SZ): Jinfollin, approved 2015 and extended to ART use in 2019; it is also developing a pre-filled pen. Group revenue was RMB13.47bn in 2024 (-7.55%), and growth-hormone centralised procurement is hurting.
- Market-research claims of 15-20% regional biosimilar share and "200+ centres" are unverified.
- **Missing:** fertility-specific revenue for both companies.

**Theramex Ovaleap, IBSA:** I found no 2025-26 figures. Both are missing.

### 2. IVF lab equipment and consumables

**Kitazato (368A.T)**
- Listed on TSE Prime on 25 Jun 2025, raising ¥18.76bn in the IPO.
- FY to March 2026: sales ¥10.95bn (+6.3%), operating profit ¥5.86bn (about 53.5% margin), overseas sales ¥7.29bn (+7.1%).
- Market cap about ¥55bn and P/E about 13.4x, against 17.7-23.5x EV/EBIT for Cooper and Vitrolife.
- About 65% of sales come from vitrification. Reported shares: Japan 60%, Europe 70%, China 80%, India 85%. These come from an MOI Global/Latticework writeup, not the company.
- Company claims more than 2.5m clinical cases a year across 115+ countries and about 3,000 centres.
- US growth runs through a new distributor, DeviMed. **Missing:** US share.
- **Moat:** decades of outcome data, lab procedures built around it, and a tiny cost relative to the cycle price.
- **Risks:** a small, founder-legacy company; currency; automated vitrification (for example Genea's Gavi) or IVM changing the workflow.

**Vitrolife (VITR.ST)**
- Q2 2026: sales SEK 857m (-2% reported, +1% organic). Consumables were a record SEK 358m (+3%; Americas consumables +15%). Technologies +1%, Genetics -2%.
- Gross margin 60.3%; EBITDA margin 34.4% (up from 27.8%); EPS SEK 0.95.
- FY2025 organic growth: Consumables +8%, Technologies +1%, Genetics +3%.
- December 2025: SEK 5.4bn goodwill impairment on Igenomix, with about 6% of staff cut and SEK 65m of annual savings (full effect by end of Q3 2026). CEO is Bronwyn Brophy O'Connor.
- Market cap about SEK 11.7bn (about $1.39bn) at about SEK 85-87 per share. EV/EBITDA about 18x, forward P/E about 31x. Consensus target is about SEK 129 with a Buy rating. These are aggregator figures and may be stale.
- EmbryoScope is used in about two-thirds of the world's time-lapse IVF cycles (company claim). The time-lapse device market was about $191.5m in 2025 (market research).
- **Biggest risk:** genetics drag, and soft demand for capital equipment.

**CooperSurgical (COO)**
- Q3 FY2026 (to July 2026): fertility revenue $141.2m (+3% reported, +5% organic), driven by genomics and lab-management platforms. CooperSurgical total $349.2m. Q4 FY2025 fertility was $141.0m (+1%).
- FY2026 guidance: CooperSurgical $1.399-1.413bn, with fertility expected to grow high single digits. That implies about $560m of fertility revenue a year (my calculation).
- The strategic review started in December 2025 and ended on 9 Sep 2026 with **no sale** of CooperSurgical and a $3bn buyback authorisation. The board blamed a new competitor in non-hormonal IUDs (a threat to Paragard) and the fertility litigation settlement.
- JANA Partners responded around 18 Sep 2026, calling for a CEO search and a sale of the fertility and medical-device assets.
- **Litigation:** in December 2023 CooperSurgical recalled three lots of LifeGlobal embryo culture media. By 4 Jun 2026 there were more than 140 suits and more than 1,500 claimants. Settlements have been "largely covered by insurance". **Missing:** settlement amounts.

**Others**
- **Hamilton Thorne:** taken private by **Astorg, not Patient Square**. It closed on 29 Nov 2024 at C$2.25 per share and was combined with Cook Medical Reproductive Health. Its brands include Gynemed, Planer, IVFtech and Embryotech.
- **Esco Medical:** bought Allwin Medical Devices in April 2026.
- **Genea:** one report says it sold for more than $250m, but I could not verify the date or buyer. Genea Biomedx (Geri, Gavi) remains a separate company.
- **FUJIFILM Irvine Scientific, Nidacon:** no financials found.

### 3. Genetic testing

PGT is **not used in egg freezing** until the eggs are thawed, fertilised and cultured.
- **Natera** discontinued Spectrum PGT-A in December 2025. A class action filed on 2 Jun 2026 alleges it was misleadingly marketed.
- **Igenomix (Vitrolife):** SEK 5.4bn impairment, with lower expected market growth cited.
- **CooperSurgical:** genomics was a Q3 FY2026 growth driver.
- **Juno Genetics (IVI RMA/US Fertility):** has tested hundreds of thousands of embryos.
- **Orchid:** whole-genome embryo screening.
- **Nucleus Genomics:** IVF+ launched in 2025 and in 2026 expanded to 160 clinics in India and the Middle East.
- **Moat:** low to medium. Evidence on PGT-A is contested, lawsuits are a risk, and the service is being commoditised.

### 4. Cryogenics

- **MVE:** Chart sold it to **Cryoport (CYRX)** for $320m, closing 1 Oct 2020. Cryoport's Life Sciences Products (MVE) had Q1 2026 revenue of $20.9m (+15%) and H1 2026 revenue of $41.9m (vs $39.3m). Cryoport sold its CRYOPDP courier unit to DHL on 11 Jun 2025.
- **Worthington:** its life-sciences cryogenics business (ex-Taylor-Wharton Cryoscience) went to **IC Biomedical** in December 2020. **WOR has no IVF cryo exposure.**
- **Thermo Fisher, PHC, Linde, Air Liquide:** IVF is immaterial to each of them.
- **TMRW:** FDA-cleared automated platform for egg and embryo storage. It has raised $105m and a $28m Series D (dates unverified). Clinic customers include CCRM and Boston IVF.
- **Risk to clinics:** tank failures, such as Cleveland and SF Pacific Fertility in March 2018. This favours paying for monitoring and automation.

### 5. AI and new technology

- **Fairtility:** CHLOE Blast became the first FDA-cleared machine-learning IVF decision-support tool in September 2025. CHLOE OQ scores oocytes, which matters for egg freezing.
- **Future Fertility:** VIOLET/MAGENTA egg-quality reports.
- **Alife:** Embryo Predict (FDA-cleared), rolled out across First Fertility in July 2026.
- **Gameto Fertilo (in vitro maturation using ovarian support cells):**
  - FDA IND cleared in January 2025; Phase 3 at 15 US sites; interim readout expected late 2026.
  - Cleared in Australia, Japan, Argentina, Paraguay, Mexico and Peru; first birth in December 2024 (Peru).
  - It needs a much shorter stimulation, so it is the most direct threat to drug revenue per cycle.
- **Conception Bio:** in July 2026 reported early human egg cells grown from stem cells (from blood, via lab-grown mini-ovaries). This is far from clinical use and not a threat before 2030.
- **Oral stimulation:**
  - Oral FSH-receptor agonists TOP5300/TOP5668 are preclinical.
  - An oral GnRH antagonist (linzagolix) study starts in September 2026. It threatens injectable ganirelix and Cetrotide.

### Bottlenecks and concentration

1. **Kitazato vitrification:** most of the market outside the US. The biggest single-supplier dependency in egg freezing.
2. **US FSH is held by three suppliers** (Merck, Ferring, Organon), with Gonal-f active ingredient made at a single site in Tres Cantos.
3. **Culture media:** the Cooper LifeGlobal recall showed how one bad lot can affect many clinics at once.
4. **Price, not supply, is the drug risk:** TrumpRx is resetting US cash prices.
