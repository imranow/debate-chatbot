## (b) Device-count model

**How the model works**
- The base unit is treated **patient-years**, not "users". Unique users are higher than patient-years because many patients stop. As a rule of thumb, patient-years ≈ 0.7-0.8 × annual unique users.
- Units per patient-year by format:
  - Weekly single-dose autoinjector (AI): 52 AIs. Each AI contains one 1 mL glass prefilled syringe (PFS).
  - Weekly 4-dose pen (KwikPen/FlexTouch): 13 pens, 13 cartridges and 52 pen needles.
  - Daily liraglutide pen: about 40 pens (blend of Victoza 1.2-1.8 mg and Saxenda 3 mg) and 365 needles.
  - Zepbound single-dose vial: 52 vials and 52 syringes.
  - Compounded multi-dose vial: about 13 vials and 52 syringes.
  - Monthly injection: 12 devices.
  - Oral: no devices.

**2026 base: about 30.8M patient-years**

This is my own estimate, implied from revenue. US tirzepatide is about 6.5M: US $9.7B per quarter at about $500/month. Ex-US Mounjaro is about 6M, Novo International Operations semaglutide about 7M, and the US about 14M in total, which cross-checks with JPM and IQVIA.

## (c) Format risk

**1. Shift from single-dose AI to 4-dose pens (US): the largest near-term risk to AI counts**
- **Zepbound KwikPen** (4 doses, 2.5-15 mg) was FDA-approved and launched on **23 Feb 2026**. It is priced the same as vials on LillyDirect: $299 / $399 / $449 a month. Kroger and retail self-pay followed in March 2026.
- **The Medicare Bridge covers only the Zepbound KwikPen.** The single-dose pen and the vial are excluded, so the fastest-growing US channel structurally uses 13 devices a year instead of 52.
- **Wegovy multi-dose FlexTouch** (9.6 mg/3 mL, four 2.4 mg doses) shows as FDA-approved and a US launch was reported as "weeks away" in Aug 2026. This comes from a Substack; there is no Novo press release.
- A Wegovy single-dose PFS was reportedly approved in May 2026.
- The shift favours in-house pen assembly by Lilly and Novo, cartridge makers (Schott, Stevanato, Gerresheimer) and pen needles (Embecta, BD). It hurts contract autoinjector volume per patient.
- ATS disclosed "lower levels of GLP-1-related activity" in autoinjector assembly-line orders (FY26 Q4 and FY27 Q1), which is consistent with this shift.

**2. Oral: a real risk, but so far it adds patients**
- Wegovy pill launched 5 Jan 2026. It had >5M cumulative prescriptions by early Aug and is about 35% of Wegovy prescriptions, but more than 80% of its patients are new to GLP-1s.
- Foundayo was approved 1 Apr 2026, shipped from 6 Apr, and is filed in more than 40 countries including the EU and Japan. The type 2 diabetes submission to FDA was planned by end-Q2 2026. Its uptake is slower: $98M in Q2.
- Oral starter-dose price is $149/month under the TrumpRx deal.
- Base case: 25% oral volume share in 2030; bear case 35%.
- The damage is mostly to growth in new starts, not to the installed injectable base. Leerink's view is that patients who need large weight loss stay on injections.

**3. Monthly: low risk before 2029**
- MariTide MARITIME-1 and MARITIME-2 primary readouts are due in **early 2027**, which implies a filing in 2027 and a launch around 2028 at the earliest.
- MariTide's device format is **unknown**.
- Monthly dosing cuts devices per patient 4.3×, from 52 to 12. I model 3% share in 2030 (8% in the bear case).
- Pfizer/Metsera monthly assets are not researched (a gap).

**4. Vials and compounded: shrinking**
- FDA proposed on **30 Apr 2026** to remove semaglutide, tirzepatide and liraglutide from the 503B bulks list. When finalised, that closes the last large-scale compounding route.
- FDA sent 55 warning letters to telehealth firms in 2026 (30 in March, 25 in June).
- Hims settled with Novo on 9 Mar 2026 and now sells branded Ozempic and Wegovy.
- The Zepbound vial's price advantage ended in Feb 2026, when KwikPen was priced the same.
- Net effect: vials and loose syringes grow at only about 7% a year in my model. Upside only if 503B is not finalised.

**5. Generic semaglutide: adds pens and needles, not autoinjectors**

| India | Launched 21 Mar 2026 by Dr Reddy's (Obeda), Sun, Zydus, Alkem, Glenmark and others at INR 1,800-4,200/month | All multi-dose disposable pens. Embecta needles are co-packaged. Shaily (India) is cited as the main generic pen maker (unverified) |

| China | Patent expired 20 Mar 2026, but no approval as of late April: 10 applications suspended on data protection. First approvals (Jiuyuan/Huadong) expected around H1 2027 | |

- The US has no generics before about 2031-32 (not verified).

**6. Pipeline timing**
- **Retatrutide:** TRIUMPH-4 read out Dec 2025 (-28.7%); TRIUMPH-1 on 21 May 2026 (-28.3% at 80 weeks); TRIUMPH-2 and -3 on 23 Jul 2026. BLA planned for **Q1 2027** (secondary source), so a US launch around 2028. Device format unknown; Lilly's pattern suggests KwikPen ex-US and possibly KwikPen in the US.
- **CagriSema:** filed with FDA **Dec 2025**; decision expected around late 2026.
- **Wegovy HD 7.2 mg:** US launch targeted for April 2026 (format not verified).

**7. Pricing and elasticity**
- Nov 2025 deals set Medicare at $245/month and TrumpRx at $350/month, trending to $245 over two years, with a $50 Medicare copay and $149 oral starter doses.
- Volume is responding: US obesity prescriptions +78%; Medicare Bridge >500k in two months; 65+ prescribing +19% month on month.
- Revenue is not keeping up: Wegovy injectable -22%; Lilly US price -9% excluding rebate adjustments.
- Read-through for suppliers: **device demand tracks volume, not drug revenue.** A drug-maker revenue slowdown can hide 20%+ growth in patient volume.
