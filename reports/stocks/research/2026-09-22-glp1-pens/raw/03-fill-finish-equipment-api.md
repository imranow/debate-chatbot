# GLP-1 / Incretin Injectables: Sterile Fill-Finish, Filling Equipment and Peptide API Supply Chain
*Sell-side style research note. As of 2026-09-22. Built from about 40 WebSearch snippets; I did not use WebFetch. Figures come from search snippets of press releases, filings and trade press. Items I did not check are flagged in section (c).*

**Bottom line:** The binding constraint has moved over time:
- **2023–24: fill-finish and device assembly.** This was the bottleneck.
- **2026: innovator fill-finish is largely freed up.** For generics and for biotechs without their own plants, the constraint is now peptide API plus access to CDMO fill capacity.
- **2028 (my estimate): API stays tighter; fill-finish faces overbuild risk.** Oral semaglutide raises peptide API demand. Lilly's and Novo's in-house fill capacity comes onstream. Small-molecule orals and monthly dosing reduce the number of pens needed.

**Listed exposure:**
- There is no listed pure play in aseptic filling machines. Syntegon, Groninger, Optima, IMA, Bausch+Ströbel, Körber, Steriline and Fedegari are all private.
- The listed proxies are Stevanato (STVN), Schott Pharma (1SXP.DE), ATS (ATS.TO), Bachem (BANB.SW), PolyPeptide (PPGN.SW, now being acquired), Lonza (LONN.SW), Samsung Biologics (207940.KS), WuXi AppTec (TIDES unit) and Gland Pharma.

## (b) Detail with sourced figures

### 1. Fill-finish capacity

**Novo Holdings / Catalent**
- Novo Holdings closed the Catalent deal on **18 Dec 2024** at about **$16.5bn EV**, all cash.
- Novo Nordisk then bought the **Anagni (IT), Bloomington (IN) and Brussels (BE)** fill-finish sites. They employ more than 3,000 people and are specialised in sterile filling.
- The upfront payment was **$11bn**. It took Novo's fill-finish footprint from 11 to 14 sites.
- Capacity was guided to rise gradually "from 2026 and onwards".
- Novo guided a low single-digit negative effect on operating-profit growth in 2024 and 2025. Catalent-related D&A and about **DKK 8bn** of restructuring weighed on 2026 guidance.

**Novo, other projects**
- **Clayton NC:** $4.1bn fill-finish plant, 1.4m sq ft, 1,000 jobs (June 2024). This is the largest life-science investment in North Carolina's history.
- **Kalundborg:** more than DKK 42bn from 2023, mostly API, completing gradually from end-2025 to 2029.
- **Hillerød:** DKK 15.9bn multi-product API plant for 2029, plus a DKK 2.9bn QC lab for 2027.
- **Odense:** DKK 8.5bn, for rare disease.
- **Capex:** about DKK 60bn in 2025 and about DKK 55bn guided for 2026, "expected to decline" after that.

**Lilly (parenteral and device sites)**
- **Concord NC:** more than $1bn, parenteral and device. Lilly later raised the amount (not verified).
- **RTP NC:** an additional $450m for parenteral filling, device assembly and packaging.
- **Kinsale IE:** $800m expansion, already producing.
- **Alzey DE:** $2.5bn parenteral and device site. With it Lilly will have six European sites, including **Fegersheim FR** (no Fegersheim dollar figure found).
- **Pleasant Prairie WI:** bought from Nexus in 2024 for an undisclosed price (84k sq ft, Nexus had spent more than $100m), plus a **$3bn expansion** for GLP-1 drug product.
- **Lehigh Valley PA:** more than $3.5bn injectable and device plant for retatrutide and next-generation drugs. Announced 30 Jan 2026; construction 2026, **operational 2031**.
- **API sites:** Lebanon IN ($9bn), Houston ($6.5bn, orforglipron), Huntsville AL ($6bn), Goochland VA ($5bn, bioconjugates).
- **Total:** more than $50bn in US commitments since 2020.
- **Production:** at least 1.6x the saleable incretin doses in H1 2025 versus H1 2024.
- **Outsourcing:** Lilly reportedly contracted **National Resilience and BSP Pharmaceuticals** for Mounjaro/Zepbound fill-finish.

### 2. Filling, isolator and inspection equipment

**Syntegon**
- 2025 revenue **€1.75bn (+10%)**, order intake **€1.86bn**, Pharma sales +22%.
- H1 2026: record backlog **€1.3bn**, book-to-bill 1.09, Pharma +14%.
- Its gloveless SynTiso line ships for the first time from 2026.
- In March 2026 Apollo bought 37% at up to **€4bn** valuation, after a full sale stalled at an asking price above €4bn.

**Other machine makers**
- **Bausch+Ströbel** is **not** part of Syntegon. The Bullinger and Ströbel families have owned it since 2007.
- **Groninger:** a single line is about 50m units a year (the Grand River Aseptic Manufacturing line example). It sells "fastlane" pre-designed lines to cut delivery times. No month figure given.
- **Vanrx SA25** (Cytiva): gloveless robotic isolator, nested vials, syringes and cartridges.

**Lead times**
- Trade sources say **18–24 months** for a new high-speed line and **12–15 months** for semi-bespoke isolators (2025).
- A new sterile facility takes **3–5 years** from groundbreaking to validation.
- The 24–36 month figure in the brief was **not confirmed**.

**Assembly and inspection cycle is turning down**
- ATS backlog was **$1,958m at 31 Mar 2026, –8.5% YoY**, with FY26 revenue of $2.97bn. Life-sciences bookings are diversifying beyond GLP-1 autoinjector lines.
- Stevanato Engineering revenue fell **–31%** in Q1 2026 (assembly and glass-converting lines down), partly offset by growth in visual inspection.

**RTU containers**
- **Stevanato** Q2 2026:
  - Revenue 302m (+8%); the snippet says USD but it is probably EUR. Adjusted EBITDA margin 26%.
  - High-value solutions +16%, now 45% of revenue. GLP-1 is about 22–23% of revenue.
  - New RTU 400 cartridge line at Latina, with commercial output in 2027.
  - Fishers IN: first EZ-fill vial line in performance qualification; device commercial production later in 2026.
  - Forward P/E about 19.5; EV/EBITDA about 22.7 (Dec 2025).
- **Schott Pharma** Q3 FY26: revenue **€282m, +10%** (8.3% at constant currency). FY26 guide raised to **5–6%** constant currency, adjusted EBITDA margin 27–28%, citing sustained demand for GLP-1 glass syringes.

### 3. Sterilisation, lyophilisation and Tyvek
- GLP-1 pens are liquid-filled, so lyophilisation matters little except for some biotech formats.
- DuPont: Tyvek 1073B/1059B production is moving to new flash-spinning lines at Richmond VA and Luxembourg. It also expanded its sterile-packaging site in Heredia, Costa Rica (June 2025). There is no GLP-1 disclosure, and it is a small share of DuPont's revenue.
- I found no data on EtO sterilisation of nests and tubs (section c).

### 4. Peptide API (upstream)

**Bachem**
- H1 2026 sales **CHF 326.4m, +4% (+7.3% in local currency)**; EBITDA margin **25.4%** (–370bp).
- FY26 guide narrowed to **+35–40% in local currency** (from 35–45%), with a low-30s EBITDA margin. That implies a very large H2; it depends on Building K.
- Building K (Bubendorf) opened April 2026 and produced its first GMP material. It booked no H1 sales.
- Capex CHF 350–400m in 2026, plus Sisslerfeld (more than CHF 500m). Order books are full for 2026.

**PolyPeptide**
- 2025 revenue about **€389m, +15.6%**; EBITDA margin 11–12% (from 7.5%).
- Metabolic work is **57%** of revenue: 10 commercial and 37 development projects.
- The large-scale site at Braine-l'Alleud ran at target utilisation. Malmö ramps in 2027. Capex just over €100m.

**Chinese and other CDMOs**
- **WuXi TIDES:** 2025 revenue **RMB 11.37bn, +96%**; Q1 2026 RMB 2.38bn. Peptide synthesis (SPPS) reactor volume above 100,000L after Taixing (Sept 2025). About +40% guided for 2026.
- **Asymchem:** SPPS capacity about 30,000L (June 2025), 44,000L targeted by end-2025.
- **Ticker correction:** 688076.SS is **Jiangsu Sinopep-Allsino**, not "Noposion". Shenzhen Noposion (002215.SZ) is an agrochemicals company.
- **Hybio:** completed NMPA registration of semaglutide API.

**Oral maths (my calculation):**
- Wegovy pill: 25 mg a day = 175 mg a week, versus 2.4 mg a week injected. That is **about 73x the API per patient-week**, because under 1% is absorbed.
- The Wegovy pill was approved in Dec 2025 and **Foundayo (orforglipron)** on 1 Apr 2026.
- Oral semaglutide therefore pushes the constraint *upstream* to API. Orforglipron needs no peptide API and no sterile fill.

### Moat and risk by layer
- **Moat:**
  - Sterile qualification, Annex 1 compliance and container-drug filing lock-in.
  - 3–5 years for a new site.
  - Equipment makers keep a service and spares annuity on a validated installed base.
- **Biggest risks:**
  1. Lilly and Novo insourcing (Novo owns Catalent's GLP-1 sites; Lilly has more than $50bn of its own plants).
  2. Small-molecule orals (orforglipron) and monthly dosing (MariTide) cutting pen units.
  3. Generic semaglutide price deflation. India had more than 40 firms and 50+ brands expected.
  4. Equipment order cyclicality, already visible at ATS and in Stevanato Engineering.
